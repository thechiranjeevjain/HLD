# MiniKV — Teaching & Life Analogies

---

## Explain to a 5-Year-Old

Imagine a toy box.

When you put a toy in:

- You also write its name in a notebook

If the box breaks:

- The notebook remembers

Some toys are only for today.
Every night, you clean broken toys.

Your favorite toy stays on top,
but the notebook is the truth.

This program is a careful toy box.

---

## Life Analogies

- WAL → writing things down
- Cache → motivation (fast, unreliable)
- Store → habits (slow, durable)
- TTL → impermanence
- Cleanup → letting go

Life works like systems:
If you trust cache over truth, you break.
